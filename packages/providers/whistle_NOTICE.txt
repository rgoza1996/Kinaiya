The pinned opaque binary comes from the cactus_needle 3.3.3 wheel at revision
d6c7d6a2e9655505e0f8e23339356dd1705900eb; that wheel contains no LICENSE/NOTICE
member. Pinned bytes do not establish full source/build/ABI equivalence or
independently resolve binary redistribution terms. No binary or weights are
redistributed in this package. See whistle_manifest.json for all identities.

The optional native streaming binding validates bounded committed/pending output,
limits contiguous PCM16 input and refuses engine reuse after uncertain dispatch.
Native stop is an explicit synchronous flush, not cancellation or user-input
commitment. The transport/client integration is tracked separately in issue93.
