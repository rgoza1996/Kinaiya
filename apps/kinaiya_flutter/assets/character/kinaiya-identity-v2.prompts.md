# Kinaiya character refresh

The seated idle atlas was edited with OpenAI's built-in image generation on
2026-11-01 from `kinaiya-seated-idle-v1.png`, using the owner's updated
Penpot Home and Options designs as visual references. The requested edit removed
hair ornaments, added white moonlit streaks and the small chest mole, preserved
the existing four-frame atlas and transparent background, and refined the back
of her head to keep it proportionate. The generated PNG is retained unmodified.

The refreshed expression and sleeping scene assets are described in
`../design/README.md`. The older core and activity atlases retain their original
identity until replaced.
